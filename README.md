# Electric Vehicle Market & Pricing Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing how brand, model, pricing, battery capacity, driving range, charging speed, performance, market segment, and customer ratings shape the EV market.

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Sales & Market Overview**: units sold, sales revenue, top brands and countries, and units sold by year
2. **Pricing & Value Analysis**: average price by market segment, top brands by average price, price per mile, and customer and safety ratings
3. **Performance & Value Analysis**: range, battery capacity, charging speed, and horsepower by model, plus price vs. range comparisons

## Key Observations
- EV pricing varies significantly between brands and market segments.
- A higher price does not always mean proportionally better range.
- Price per Mile helps identify models that offer more driving range for their price.
- Market segmentation strongly influences pricing, specifications, and customer choice.

## Analysis Covers
Sales performance, average price and revenue, driving range and battery capacity, charging performance, brand price comparisons, segment price differences, price vs. horsepower and other specs, best-value models, customer ratings, and breakdowns by body type, drive type, country, and seating capacity.
